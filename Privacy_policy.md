# Warden™ — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 25 October 2026  
**Operator:** Starlit community  
**Official community/support:** https://discord.gg/starlittt

## 1. Scope and Starlit-only operation

Warden™ is operated exclusively for the **Starlit™ Discord server**. It is not intended to manage, moderate, or collect data for any other Discord server. This Policy explains how information may be processed when Warden™ operates in Starlit, including when members interact with its commands or when staff use its moderation and security features.

## 2. Information that may be processed

Depending on the features enabled and how the deployed code is configured, Warden™ may process:
- **Discord user IDs** to identify users and associate actions or settings with the correct account.
- **Starlit server ID and server settings** needed to identify the server and apply its configuration.
- **Moderation and security records**, such as actions, reports, incidents, or related logs, where those features are enabled.
- **Command usage and error logs** used to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the bot.

This list reflects the categories supplied by the operator; the exact fields retained depend on the deployed code and provider configuration. The operator should verify that the running bot does not collect additional categories.

## 3. How information may be used

Information may be used to:
- Provide and maintain Warden™'s functions within Starlit.
- Apply Starlit's moderation and security settings and help staff review incidents.
- Troubleshoot errors, monitor reliability, and investigate abuse or technical problems.
- Protect the bot, Starlit, and their users from misuse or unauthorized access.
- Respond to requests concerning data, safety, or operation of the bot.

## 4. Exclusive server limitation

Warden™ is intended to process server-specific configuration and activity only for Starlit. It is not designed or offered as a public bot for other Discord communities. If the bot's deployment or code changes to support another server, this Policy must be reviewed and updated before that change is put into use.

## 5. Storage and security

Information may be stored in the bot's database, logs, hosting environment, or related infrastructure, depending on the deployed implementation. The operator should use reasonable access controls and security practices to protect stored information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 6. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described in this Policy, or as needed for security, operational, or legal reasons. Some records may remain in backups or provider logs for a period determined by the relevant systems. The exact retention period and deletion behavior depend on the deployed code and hosting/database providers; no fixed deletion schedule is promised here.

You may contact the Starlit community to ask about information associated with you or request deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained temporarily for security, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. The operator should verify that any deletion request can be fulfilled across the bot's database, logs, and relevant provider systems.

## 7. Sharing and third-party services

Warden™ may rely on:
- **Discord**, to provide the platform through which the bot operates.
- **Hosting and database providers**, to run the bot and store or process its data.

Information may be processed by these providers as necessary to operate the service. Their own terms, privacy policies, and technical practices apply to their systems. The operator should list any additional APIs, analytics, monitoring, or integrations if they are added to the production deployment.

## 8. International processing

Discord or the hosting/database providers may process information in locations different from yours. The operator should check the providers' current data-location and transfer terms and make any disclosures required by applicable law.

## 9. Your requests and choices

For privacy questions or requests to access, correct, or delete information associated with you, contact the Starlit community at https://discord.gg/starlittt. The operator will review requests and respond as required by applicable law and technical capability. Discord account data and information held by Discord itself are subject to Discord's own processes.

## 10. Children's privacy

Warden™ follows the age requirements applicable to Discord and the relevant services. It is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe information has been processed improperly, contact the Starlit community.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when Warden™, Starlit, the bot's data practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date above should be updated when a revised version takes effect.

## 12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the Starlit community through https://discord.gg/starlittt.
